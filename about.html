About airbnb-india.com

We believe the soul of Indian travel lives in its homestays — and finding the right one shouldn't be a gamble.

Our Story

airbnb-india.com started with a simple frustration: every travel search threw up a thousand anonymous hotel listings, while the best homestays — the ones with the sun-drenched verandas, the grandmother's cooking, the orchard views — stayed hidden behind generic pages.

So we built the directory we wished existed: a city-wise, state-wise guide to India's best bed & breakfasts, homestays, villas, cottages and houseboats. Every property is handpicked, written up properly, and linked directly to its Airbnb listing so travellers can book securely in one click.

What We Do

  • Curate: we review guest ratings, host response and recent reviews before recommending any stay.
  • Organise: everything is sorted by destination and state so you can plan around places, not algorithms.
  • Connect: we hand travellers straight to the official Airbnb listing for live pricing and protected booking.
  • Promote hosts: Indian homestay owners get a beautiful, SEO-friendly page — free for a limited time.

Our Values

Honesty. We don't accept payment for inflated ratings or fake reviews. Local first. The majority of our listed properties are run by Indian families and small hosts. Free for a limited time. Listing your BNB is free for one listing per host/BNB while the offer lasts.
