Best Time to Visit Gangtok

The best time to visit Gangtok is October to June, when the skies are clear and the Gangtok weather is pleasant. Weekends and school holidays book out fast, so reserve early if you plan to stay on a popular long weekend.

How to Reach Gangtok

Fly or take a train to Gangtok's nearest hub, then a short taxi, auto or local bus gets you into town. Most Gangtok hosts send precise directions after you book. If you drive, most of our listed stays offer parking — check each listing for details.
