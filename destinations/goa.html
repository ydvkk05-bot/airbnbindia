Best Time to Visit Goa

The best time to visit Goa is October to March. Weekends and school holidays book out fast, so reserve early if you plan to stay on a popular long weekend.

How to Reach Goa

Fly or take a train to Goa's nearest hub, then a short taxi, auto or local bus gets you into town. Most Goa hosts send precise directions after you book. If you drive, most of our listed stays offer parking — check each listing for details.
