Villa Gecko Dorado is part of an 18th. C. Heritage Portuguese house. Set in a serene yet vibrant tropical flowering garden, the villa with it's own private entrance is a chic and unique living space. It's lavish interiors are themed around an eclectic mix of modernity with a combination of strong artistic influences.
